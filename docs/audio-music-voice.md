## ACE-Step 1.5 and XL

The registry separates 2B and XL, then Base, SFT, and Turbo. Prompt packages can represent lyrics, duration, BPM, key, time signature, language, and references.

## Qwen3-TTS

Base, CustomVoice, and VoiceDesign serve different tasks. Voice-clone references and derived speaker prompts must stay tied to the model that created them.

## Stable Audio 3

The profile distinguishes music, sound-effect, medium, and API-oriented variants. Stable Audio 3 must use its matching latent and autoencoder system.

## Rights

Voice and audio reference manifests contain rights and consent fields. Unknown rights should not be silently upgraded to approved.
