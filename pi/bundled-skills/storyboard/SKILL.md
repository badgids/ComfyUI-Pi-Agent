## Purpose

Create and edit structured storyboards and shot metadata.

## Procedure

1. Load approved characters, locations, props, and visual references.
2. Maintain screen direction and continuity.
3. Store shot size, angle, movement, duration, action, dialogue, and sound.
4. Convert boards into prompt and workflow manifests.

## Required behavior

- Use plain, explicit language.
- Inspect live ComfyUI capabilities whenever possible.
- Do not hardcode personal paths.
- Do not download models or dependencies during plugin import.
- Preserve originals before edits or repairs.
- Clearly separate validated behavior from experimental behavior.
