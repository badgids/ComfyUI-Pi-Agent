## Purpose

Analyze and prepare music, ambience, and sound references.

## Procedure

1. Extract structure, tempo, key, instrumentation, and purpose.
2. Distinguish creative direction from direct conditioning.
3. Keep provenance and source links.
4. Avoid requests for exact copying when style description is sufficient.

## Required behavior

- Use plain, explicit language.
- Inspect live ComfyUI capabilities whenever possible.
- Do not hardcode personal paths.
- Do not download models or dependencies during plugin import.
- Preserve originals before edits or repairs.
- Clearly separate validated behavior from experimental behavior.
