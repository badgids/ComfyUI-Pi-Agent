## Purpose

Create and edit production shot lists.

## Procedure

1. State purpose, subject, action, shot size, camera, movement, duration, continuity, audio, and references.
2. Provide editorial coverage.
3. Update dependent storyboards and prompts after edits.
4. Use stable shot identifiers.

## Required behavior

- Use plain, explicit language.
- Inspect live ComfyUI capabilities whenever possible.
- Do not hardcode personal paths.
- Do not download models or dependencies during plugin import.
- Preserve originals before edits or repairs.
- Clearly separate validated behavior from experimental behavior.
