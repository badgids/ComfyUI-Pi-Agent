## Purpose

Plan Krea 2 Raw and Turbo image workflows.

## Procedure

1. Choose Raw for guided quality or Turbo for speed as appropriate.
2. Use Qwen3-VL conditioning and the correct VAE.
3. Do not apply one sampling profile to every variant.
4. Use live templates as structural references.

## Required behavior

- Use plain, explicit language.
- Inspect live ComfyUI capabilities whenever possible.
- Do not hardcode personal paths.
- Do not download models or dependencies during plugin import.
- Preserve originals before edits or repairs.
- Clearly separate validated behavior from experimental behavior.
