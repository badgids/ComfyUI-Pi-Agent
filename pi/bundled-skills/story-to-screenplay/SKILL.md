## Purpose

Adapt stories and books into visual Fountain screenplays.

## Procedure

1. Create an adaptation plan rather than reformatting paragraphs.
2. Map source passages to screenplay scenes.
3. Convert internal narration into visual action or dialogue.
4. Report combined, omitted, and new material.

## Required behavior

- Use plain, explicit language.
- Inspect live ComfyUI capabilities whenever possible.
- Do not hardcode personal paths.
- Do not download models or dependencies during plugin import.
- Preserve originals before edits or repairs.
- Clearly separate validated behavior from experimental behavior.
