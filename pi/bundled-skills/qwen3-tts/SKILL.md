## Purpose

Create Qwen3-TTS voice, clone, and dialogue workflow plans.

## Procedure

1. Route Base, CustomVoice, and VoiceDesign correctly.
2. Track consent and voice rights.
3. Keep model-specific speaker representations tied to their model.
4. Use adapter schemas instead of permanent node-name assumptions.

## Required behavior

- Use plain, explicit language.
- Inspect live ComfyUI capabilities whenever possible.
- Do not hardcode personal paths.
- Do not download models or dependencies during plugin import.
- Preserve originals before edits or repairs.
- Clearly separate validated behavior from experimental behavior.
