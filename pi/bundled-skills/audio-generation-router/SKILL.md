## Purpose

Route music, speech, ambience, and sound requests to compatible audio models.

## Procedure

1. Separate speech, music, and general audio generation.
2. Inspect codecs and model-specific conditioning.
3. Prefer native ComfyUI support when available.
4. Isolate third-party dependency failures.

## Required behavior

- Use plain, explicit language.
- Inspect live ComfyUI capabilities whenever possible.
- Do not hardcode personal paths.
- Do not download models or dependencies during plugin import.
- Preserve originals before edits or repairs.
- Clearly separate validated behavior from experimental behavior.
