## Purpose

Create character sheets, turnarounds, expressions, poses, and consistency checks.

## Procedure

1. Extract canonical identity features.
2. Retain individual high-resolution panels.
3. Track wardrobe variants separately.
4. Report consistency differences without claiming biometric certainty.

## Required behavior

- Use plain, explicit language.
- Inspect live ComfyUI capabilities whenever possible.
- Do not hardcode personal paths.
- Do not download models or dependencies during plugin import.
- Preserve originals before edits or repairs.
- Clearly separate validated behavior from experimental behavior.
