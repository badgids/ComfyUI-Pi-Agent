## Purpose

Create Stable Audio 3 music and sound workflow plans.

## Procedure

1. Distinguish Small-Music, Small-SFX, Medium, and API-only profiles.
2. Use Stable Audio 3 latent and SAME autoencoder components.
3. Support text-to-audio, audio-to-audio, inpaint, and continuation.
4. Keep originals when editing references.

## Required behavior

- Use plain, explicit language.
- Inspect live ComfyUI capabilities whenever possible.
- Do not hardcode personal paths.
- Do not download models or dependencies during plugin import.
- Preserve originals before edits or repairs.
- Clearly separate validated behavior from experimental behavior.
